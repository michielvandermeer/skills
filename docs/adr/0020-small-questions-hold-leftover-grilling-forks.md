# Small questions tickets hold leftover grilling forks

A session resolves one wayfinder ticket, except research tickets, which charting fires together so that research can burn down in parallel. A grilling question that stands alone does not become its own session. A session that kept taking tickets would blow the context window the map exists to protect.

Grilling tickets come in two shapes. A tree stays Focused. Leftover unblocked one-question forks share one Small questions ticket, even when the subjects differ, with no extra cap beyond the existing 250K session ceiling. New leftovers join that ticket while it is unclaimed. The map's one-line gist lists each decision.

## Considered Options

- Sessions keep taking grilling tickets — rejected: blows the context window.
- Cluster leftovers by subject — rejected: these questions have no shared subject; the point is to get through them fast.
- Cap a Small questions ticket at a handful of questions — rejected: the 250K ceiling is the backstop.
