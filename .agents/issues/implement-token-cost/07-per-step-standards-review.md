# Each Step runs its own Standards review

Category: enhancement
Status: needs-grilling

## Problem

Each `skills:implementer` runs `/code-review` on its own commit, both axes, at a large context. The final review in `/implement` step 4 reviews the whole branch again against the Coding standards.

Proposed change: drop the per-Step Standards review and let the final review cover it. Estimated saving: about 3% of total spend. The cost is quality: problems are caught later, and the fixer at the end gets more findings.

The full measurement is in commit `5d4d45e`, in the Issue this one was split from.
