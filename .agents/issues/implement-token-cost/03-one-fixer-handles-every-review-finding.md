# One fixer handles every final-review finding and can run very long

Category: enhancement
Status: needs-grilling

## Problem

In `/implement` step 4, one `general-purpose` fixer gets every finding from both review axes. In 48 hours of transcripts (2026-09-24 to 2026-09-26), fixers cost $122, about 9% of total spend. One fixer ran 285 turns, grew to 586k tokens of context, and cost $57 on its own.

Proposed change: split the fixer, with one fixer per review axis or one per area of code. Estimated saving: about 4% of total spend.

The full measurement is in commit `5d4d45e`, in the Issue this one was split from.
