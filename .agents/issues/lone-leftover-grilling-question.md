# A lone leftover grilling question gets a ticket of its own

Status: needs-grilling
Category: bug

`/wayfinder` groups leftover one-question grilling forks into one Small questions ticket. When only one leftover turns up and no Small questions ticket is open, `skills/wayfinder/SKILL.md` (Grilling tickets, Group step 2) gives it a ticket of its own: "a singleton keeps a title that names its fork".

ADR-0020 says "A grilling question that stands alone does not become its own session", and that leftovers "share one Small questions ticket". Its glossary entry for Small questions agrees.

Both texts came from the same commit (de2f252), so neither is clearly the mistake.

A side effect: a lone leftover's ticket is not a Small questions ticket, so a leftover found in a later round has nothing to join. Two rounds with one leftover each leave two one-question tickets, and each takes a session of its own.

This is parked because a person has to choose between two fixes:

- **Keep the skill.** A lone leftover keeps a title that names its question, which is easier to read than "Small questions". ADR-0020 then gets a sentence that allows it, and possibly a rule that a later leftover joins that ticket.
- **Keep the ADR.** A lone leftover starts a Small questions ticket, so later leftovers join it. The skill's step 2 becomes "With nothing to join, mint Small questions."

Nothing else is blocked by this.
